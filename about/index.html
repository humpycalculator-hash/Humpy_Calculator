About Humpy Calculator

One calculator, to actually explain itself.

Humpy Calculator is a free collection of math, finance, physics, chemistry, health and conversion tools that show their working, not just a final number.

01

What we do

Every calculator on this site takes your inputs and walks through the formula step by step before giving the result. You can learn the method, double-check homework, or catch a mistake before it matters.

02

Why Humpy

We were students juggling different calculator sites for one assignment, each cluttered with pop-ups and none explaining how it got its answer. Humpy is the single tool we wished existed.

03

How it works

Pick a category, choose the calculation you need, and fill in the values. The calculations run in your browser, and the calculator code does not send your inputs to our server.

Explore the tools

What's inside

Practical calculators across six categories.

Math

Percentages, square roots, exponents, logarithms, fractions, GPA and algebra.

Finance

Loan and EMI estimates, compound interest, sales tax, discounts and margins.

Physics

Speed, force, energy, free fall and Ohm's law.

Chemistry

Molar mass, molarity, pH and the ideal gas law.

Conversions

Length, weight, temperature, currency and data storage. Currency conversions use an exchange rate you enter; rates are not live.

Health

BMI, BMR, calorie needs and ideal-weight estimates.

What matters to us

Our promise

Useful tools, with clear limits and no surprises.

  • Free to use, with no sign-up.
  • Calculator inputs are processed in your browser and are not sent to our server by the calculator code.
  • Step-by-step explanations help you follow the calculation.
  • Built by students, and open to your suggestions.

Good to know

Humpy is for learning and quick checks. Check important results against an official source or a qualified professional, especially for exams or medical, legal or financial decisions. Health calculators provide general estimates, not diagnoses, and currency rates are not live.
