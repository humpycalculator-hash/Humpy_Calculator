Privacy Policy

All numbers are processed
locally on your device.

Humpy Calculator doesn't ask who you are and doesn't keep what you type. Here is exactly what the site does, and doesn't do, with your information.

Last updated: November 7, 2026

What we collect

Nothing personal. There are no accounts, sign-up forms or comment boxes, so there's nowhere to give us your name or email.

What stays on your device

Only your display choices (theme color) and, if you use the GPA calculators, your grading scale. They live in your own browser and never reach us.

What we never do

We never sell or share your data, and the numbers you type into a calculator are never sent to us.

The short version

Every calculator on Humpy runs inside your browser. When you type numbers in and press Calculate, the math happens on your own device. Those numbers aren't uploaded, saved on a server, or linked to you in any way.

What we collect

We don't collect personal information. At the time of writing, the site also doesn't use analytics tools or advertising trackers, so we don't track which calculators you open or what you enter into them.

What's stored in your browser

To remember your choices, your browser keeps a few small items in localStorage, a storage area that belongs to your own browser:

  • theme : the color theme you picked from the Theme menu.
  • humpy-grade-scale: the grading scale you set in the GPA, SGPA or CGPA calculator (grade names and grade points), only if you use it.
  • humpy-disclaimer-health and humpy-disclaimer-finance: remember that you closed the disclaimer message on the Health or Finance page. These live in sessionStorage, so they are forgotten when you close the browser tab.

These items stay on your device and we can't see them. They hold no personal information, and the numbers you type into calculators are not stored. To remove them, clear this site's data in your browser settings. The site then goes back to its defaults.

Cookies

Humpy Calculator doesn't set any cookies of its own.

Third-party services

A couple of outside services are involved, and we want to be upfront about them:

  • Google Fonts. To display our fonts (Inter), your browser downloads font files from Google. As with any web request, Google receives technical details such as your IP address and browser type. Google's own policy explains how it handles this: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Social links. The icons in our footer link to our Facebook and Instagram pages. Just visiting Humpy doesn't send anything to them, but once you click through, their privacy policies apply, not ours.

Hosting and server logs

Like most websites, the service that hosts our pages may keep basic technical logs, such as IP address, date and time, and which page was requested, for security and troubleshooting. We don't use these logs to identify or track individual visitors.

Children and students

Humpy is built by students, for students. Because we don't collect personal information from anyone, we don't knowingly collect it from children either.

Changes to this policy

If the site changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example if we add analytics, accounts or a contact form, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date before the change goes live.
