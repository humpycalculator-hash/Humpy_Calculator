Questions or ideas?
We'd like to hear.

Humpy Calculator is built by students and improved by the people who use it. Send us a suggestion, a bug report or a question.

Suggest a calculator

Missing a tool you need for study or work? Tell us what it should calculate and which formula it uses.

Report a wrong result

If an answer looks wrong, send us the inputs and the result you expected. Mistakes get fixed fast.

Ask a question

Not sure how a calculator works or what a result means? Ask and we'll explain.

Email us

The best way to reach us is by email: humpycalculator@gmail.com. We read every message and reply as soon as we can.

Reporting a wrong result

To help us find the problem quickly, please include:

  • the calculator you used (for example, Finance, Loan / EMI);
  • the exact values and units you entered;
  • the result you got, and the result you expected;
  • your device and browser, if the page looked broken.

What we can't help with

We can't give personal medical, financial, tax or legal advice, and we can't solve homework for you. For those, please speak to a qualified professional or your teacher. See our Terms of Use for details.

Security problems

If you find a security problem, please email us privately with "Security report" in the subject line and don't share the details publicly until we've had a chance to fix it.

Your privacy

We only use your email address to reply to you. Read more in our Privacy Policy.
