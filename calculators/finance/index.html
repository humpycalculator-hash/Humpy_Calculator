About the Finance calculators

These calculators help you estimate loan instalments, compound growth, tax, discounts, margins and pay. Each result comes with its formula and working, so you can see exactly how the figure was reached.

Results are estimates for planning and comparing options. Banks and tax offices may add fees, use different rounding or follow their own rules, so confirm important figures with your lender or an official source.

Tips for accurate results

  • Enter the annual interest rate as a plain percentage (12, not 0.12). The EMI calculator takes the tenure in months; the Mortgage calculator takes it in years.
  • Margin and markup are different. With cost 600 and selling price 800, the margin is 25% (profit ÷ selling price) and the markup is 33.33% (profit ÷ cost).
  • The currency menu only changes the symbol shown in the result. It does not convert money.

Frequently asked questions

What is EMI and how is it calculated?

EMI is the fixed amount you pay each month on a loan. The formula is EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n ÷ ((1 + r)^n − 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n is the number of months.

Does the loan calculator include bank fees or insurance?

No. It uses only the amount, the interest rate and the tenure. Processing fees, insurance and taxes are not included, so your bank’s own schedule can differ a little.

What does compounding frequency mean?

It is how often interest is added to the balance. Monthly compounding adds interest 12 times a year. The more often interest is added, the slightly faster the balance grows.

What is the difference between adding VAT and extracting VAT?

Adding VAT takes a price without VAT and gives the total with VAT. Extracting VAT does the opposite: it takes a total that already includes VAT and splits it into the net price and the VAT amount. For example, Rs 1,150 including 15% VAT is Rs 1,000 plus Rs 150 VAT.

Not on this page. Use the Currency tool on the Conversion page, where you enter the exchange rate yourself.
