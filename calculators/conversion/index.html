About the Unit Converters

Convert length, weight, temperature, volume, area, data storage and currency with one converter for each type. Pick the unit you have, pick the unit you want and the result appears with the factor used.

Length, weight, volume, area and data conversions use fixed standard factors. Currency is different, because exchange rates change all the time. The currency converter does not fetch live rates: you enter the rate you want to use.

Tips for accurate results

  • For currency, enter the rate as units of the target currency per 1 unit of the source currency, taken from your bank, an exchange or an official source.
  • The Weight converter includes kilograms, grams, milligrams, pounds, ounces, stones, metric tons and US tons.
  • The Area converter includes square metres, square feet, acres, hectares, square kilometres and square miles. For example, 1 acre is about 4,046.86 square metres.

Frequently asked questions

Why doesn’t the currency converter show live rates?

Live rates would need the page to contact an outside service. This site keeps every calculation inside your browser and sends nothing out, so you type in a rate instead.

How accurate are the unit conversions?

They use standard conversion factors and show up to four decimal places, so the results are accurate for everyday, school and general use.

Is 1 GB equal to 1,000 MB or 1,024 MB?

The Data Storage converter uses the decimal (SI) system, so 1 GB = 1,000 MB. Some operating systems report binary sizes (1 GiB = 1,024 MiB), so the numbers you see on your device can differ slightly.

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit or kelvin?

°F = °C × 9/5 + 32 and K = °C + 273.15. The Temperature converter does this for any pair of Celsius, Fahrenheit and kelvin.

Which units are available?

Length, weight, volume, area, data storage (bits to yottabytes and beyond), temperature (°C, °F, K) and nine currencies: USD, EUR, GBP, PKR, INR, AED, SAR, JPY and CNY.

Currency results depend on the rate you enter and are not live. For real transactions, check the rate with your bank or exchange.
